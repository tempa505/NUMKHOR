## Brand & Style
The brand embodies bespoke automotive restoration, concourse-level paint correction, and advanced ceramic/graphene coating tailored to elite collectors and automotive enthusiasts in Perth, Western Australia. The aesthetic blends high-precision technical engineering with ultra-luxury bespoke craft.

- **Design Tone:** Obsidian stealth, surgical precision, cinematic luxury, and disciplined restraint.
- **Visual Style:** Minimalist Dark Luxury infused with subtle glassmorphic elements and ultra-fine mechanical accents. The interface mirrors the surface of flawlessly polished metallic clear-coat: liquid blacks, deep carbon tones, razor-sharp specular reflections, and refined metallic accents.
- **Target Audience:** Exotic, classic, and prestige vehicle owners (Porsche, Ferrari, Aston Martin, bespoke 4WDs) expecting five-star studio treatment, total transparency, and clinical excellence.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in an abyss of carbon tones illuminated by precision luminescence and liquid metallic cues:

- **Base Surfaces:**
  - `#0B0D10` (Obsidian Base): Canvas ground, creating deep infinite blacks.
  - `#12151B` (Carbon Bed): Secondary structural containers and sections.
  - `#1A1E26` (Slate Tier): Card backgrounds, elevated modules, and sheet layers.
- **Accents & Telemetry:**
  - `#38BDF8` (Precision Cyan): Primary interactive focus, laser inspection guides, high-tech paint-depth metrics, and primary CTA glow.
  - `#D4AF37` (Liquid Amber / Concourse Gold): Secondary badge highlights, bespoke warranty tiers, and heritage seals.
  - `#E2E8F0` / `#94A3B8` (Specular Chrome & Polished Steel): Tertiary metallic accents, secondary micro-copy, and hairline separators.
- **Text & Contrast:**
  - `#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC`: Primary headings and critical stats for glare-free readability against dark tiers.
  - `#94A3B8`: Secondary descriptive text and structural technical labels.
  - `#475569`: Subdued metadata and disabled states.

## Typography
The system balances surgical automotive data with modern high-end architectural appeal:

- **Space Grotesk (Headlines):** Delivers a technical, luxury posture with crisp geometric terminations. Used for main statement titles, package banners, and editorial transitions.
- **Manrope (Body):** Delivers balanced readability, warm neutral curves, and clear rendering on OLED mobile panels.
- **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry & Labels):** Applied for micron paint depth indicators, pricing tokens, treatment timestamps, warranty tags, and technical specs. Letter spacing is systematically extended for high-definition legibility.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column layout engineered for high-resolution desktop viewports down to single-column handheld screens:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, max canvas width 1360px centered, 48px canvas margins, 24px gutters. Provides generous air around high-gloss photography and detailed inspection reports.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px canvas margins, 20px gutters. Dual-card configurations for service comparison grids.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns (or single stack), 20px canvas margins, 16px gutters. Packages switch to swipeable horizontal snaps with sticky inspection summary bars.
- **Rhythm:** Modular 8pt scale governs whitespace. Section dividers lean on vertical margins rather than solid breaks, creating an unbroken liquid journey through the studio tiers.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics physical light entering an indoor detail bay equipped with diffuse overhead LEDs:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - `Level 0 (Canvas):` `#0B0D10` solid base.
  - `Level 1 (Panels & Trays):` `#12151B` backed with a 1px border using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
  - `Level 2 (Cards & Active Modules):` `#1A1E26` at 85% opacity, combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`.
- **Specular Highlights:** Cards feature a linear border gradient from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` at the top edge descending to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` at the bottom edge, simulating ceiling LED illumination.
- **Ambient Glows:** Elevated interactive states employ targeted colored diffusion rather than standard muddy drop shadows:
  - Primary Active: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.22)`
  - Bespoke/Gold Tier: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.20)`
  - Standard Elevation: `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.70)`

## Shapes
Corner radii balance streamlined car body silhouettes with architectural structure:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Used on inner interactive inputs, segmented toggles, and detail spec badges.
- **Container Radius (1rem / 16px):** Standard for service cards, vehicle preview frames, and booking modulators.
- **Structural Radius (1.5rem / 24px):** Hero showcase blocks, before/after visualizers, and sticky quote sheets.
- **Pill Shape:** Exclusively reserved for category filter chips, status badges, and phone/emergency concierge CTAs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Precision Glow):** Background `#38BDF8`, text `#0B0D10`, font `JetBrains Mono` weight 500, letter-spacing 0.05em, uppercase. Box-shadow `0 0 20px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`. Hover elevates brightness with an expanded cyan diffuse reflection.
- **Secondary (Chrome Edge):** Background `rgba(26, 30, 38, 0.8)`, text `#F8FAFC`, hairline outer border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Hover transitions border to `#38BDF8` with text color shift.
- **Concourse / Bespoke:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #D4AF37`, text `#D4AF37`. Hover renders `#D4AF37` fill with `#0B0D10` text.

### Cards & Service Packages
- Background `#1A1E26` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- Border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)` with a top-down specular gradient.
- Inner padding: `space-xl` (desktop), `space-lg` (mobile).
- Interactive hover: Y-axis lift of -4px with border transition to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Pill shape, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`. Monospaced `label-sm` font.
- **Warranty / Gold Seal:** Pill shape, `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.10)` fill, `#D4AF37` text, and subtle golden outline.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#12151B`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#475569`.
- Focus state: Border shifts to `#38BDF8` with a subtle inner glow `0 0 0 1px #38BDF8`.
- Height: 48px to satisfy luxury touch ergonomics on mobile.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base box: 20px × 20px, `#12151B` fill, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` border, rounded to 4px (checkbox) or full circle (radio).
- Checked: Fill `#38BDF8` with an inset carbon `#0B0D10` indicator mark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Before/After Split Visualizer:** Full-bleed interactive glass slider split by a 2px hairline `#38BDF8` beam with coordinate drag handle.
- **Micron Paint Depth Gauge:** Telemetry gauge with tabular mono readings, warning threshold indicators, and glossy tick lines.
- **Vehicle Tier Selector:** Multi-segment pill selector (Supercar, Classic Coupe, Executive Sedan, Luxury SUV) featuring smooth sliding background indicators in dark metallic slate.
